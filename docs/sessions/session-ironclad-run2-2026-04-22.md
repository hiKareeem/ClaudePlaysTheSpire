## Death Analysis

**Cause**: Entered Spiny Toad fight at 13 HP with no healing opportunity.

**Contributing factors**:
1. **Path planning failure**: Prioritized (5,5) Monster over closer rest site. After the brutal 3-enemy fight at Floor 19 (ended at 7 HP), the next healing was 2 floors away.
2. **Thorns=5**: Every attack costs 5 HP. At 13 HP, only ~2 attacks possible before dying to thorns alone.
3. **Deck bloat**: 25 cards reduced consistency. Should have skipped more rewards.
4. **Event choice**: Room Full of Cheese (Gorge) added 2 mediocre cards instead of risking Search (lose 14 HP). But at 13 HP, Search was death anyway.

**What would have saved us**:
- Rest site between Floor 19 and Floor 22
- Skipping the Monster at (5,5) to take a rest site path
- Better HP management in the Floor 19 triple fight

## IPC Findings (Critical for Future Runs)

### Command Structure
- **`type` key** inside Send-BridgeCommand: `@{type='PlayCard'; handIndex=0}` (not `command`)
- **`targetIndex=0` REQUIRED** for ALL attack cards, even single-enemy. Without it: "card unplayable"
- **Self-target cards (Impervious, etc.)**: MUST NOT include targetIndex. Throws "card unplayable" if included.

### State Structure
- **Flat top-level**: `combat`, `run`, `map` are siblings of `screen`, not nested
- `screen.name` (not `.currentScreen`)
- `combat.roundNumber` (not `.round`)
- `combat.player.block` (not `.playerBlock`)
- Hand cards: **FLAT in combat** — `$card.title` works, no `.card` wrapper
- Grid/reward cards: **Nested** under `.card` wrapper

### Timing & Bugs
- **Post-EndTurn lies**: State shows stale round/0 energy. Wait 4–5 seconds, re-read.
- **Hand indices shift** after every PlayCard — MUST re-read state before each card play.
- **CardGridSelection auto-resolves**: SelectCardsInGrid throws "task already completed" but selection goes through.
- **Headbutt "return from discard"** triggers CardGridSelection showing full discard pile.
- **Armaments "upgrade in hand"** triggers HandSelect with mode=UpgradeSelect.
- **Brand "exhaust 1 card"** triggers HandConfirmSelect with mode=SimpleSelect.
- **Potion reward skip broken**: Must accept potions to proceed (auto-skip when full, game proceeds to Map).
- **Happy Flower**: May give bonus energy at unexpected times (round 1?). Verify energy before playing.
- **Reward selection**: Use `rewards[i].index` (not array position `i`).
- **Reward kind field**: `kind` (not `type`): Gold, Potion, Relic, Card.
- **Card rewards**: Cards nested under `cardRewardOptions.cards[].card`.
- **Powers on enemies**: Use `.title` (not `.name`).
- **Map nodes (Act 2)**: `pointType` (not `roomType`), nodes have `children` for connections.
- **Impervious block**: Showed 22 instead of expected 30. Unknown game mechanic (possibly affected by something).
- **Rapid sequential PlayCard**: Commands can be lost. One card per state update.

## Strategy Lessons for Future Runs

1. **HP > card rewards > gold.** Never enter a fight below 30% HP unless it's the boss.
2. **Rest sites are mandatory** after hard fights. Count rest sites in path planning.
3. **Skip mediocre card rewards.** Deck bloat kills consistency. Aim for 20 cards max by Act 2.
4. **Thorns enemies**: Only attack with high-damage cards. Avoid low-value Strikes.
5. **Strength engine (Brand)**: Powerful but requires surviving to stack it. Don't sacrifice HP for Str.
6. **Silver Crucible**: Excellent Neow pick — 3 free upgrades early is massive.
7. **Sand Castle (Orobas)**: 6 random upgrades is great for decks with many base cards.
8. **Anchor relic**: 10 starting block is consistently useful. Don't underestimate it.
9. **Block-first combat**: Always calculate incoming damage and ensure enough block. Damage races fail.
10. **Know when to tank**: Sometimes eating damage to kill an enemy faster is correct. Calculate break-even.
