- **Byrd Swoop** [Colorless Attack] — Deal 14 damage.
- **Caltrops** [Silent Power] — Whenever you are attacked, deal 3 damage back.
- **Clash** [Ironclad Attack] — Can only be played if every card in your Hand is an Attack. Deal 14 damage.

- **Feeding Frenzy** [Colorless Skill] — Gain 5 Strength this turn.

- **Mad Science** [Colorless Skill] — This card can be created and customized at the event Tinker Time.
- **Metamorphosis** [Colorless Skill] — Add 3 random Attacks into your Draw Pile. They're free to play this combat. Exhaust.
- **Outmaneuver** [Silent Skill] — Next turn, gain 2 Energy.
- **Peck** [Colorless Attack] — Deal 2 damage 3 times.
- **Rebound** [Defect Attack] — Deal 9 damage. Put the next card you play this turn on top of your Draw Pile.
- **Rip and Tear** [Defect Attack] — Deal 7 damage to a random enemy twice.
- **Squash** [Colorless Attack] — Deal 10 damage. Apply 2 Vulnerable.
- **Stack** [Defect Skill] — Gain Block equal to the number of cards in your Discard Pile.
- **Toric Toughness** [Colorless Skill] — Gain 5 Block. Gain 5 Block at the start of the next 2 turns.
